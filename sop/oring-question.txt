# Same insight as oring-conditional, but WITHOUT status words, so it carries none of
# the 0-row risk that instructing PASSED/MISSING may have caused in oring-strict.
#
# An interrogative asserts nothing — there is no claim for the model to restate as an
# observation — while leaving the verdict vocabulary entirely to the system message,
# which already specifies PASSED/FAILED/MISSING and how to choose between them.
#
# If this detects the omission and the five declarative variants did not, the finding
# is that SOP steps must be written as questions rather than as statements, which is
# a concrete and cheap thing to tell a customer.
Step 1: Does the worker attach, place, or align the black O-ring onto the blue cap?
Step 2: Does the worker attach, insert, or align the blue cap into a port of the black manifold by hand?
Step 3: Does the worker use a wrench to tighten the blue cap?
