Step 1: Focus on what the worker has in their hands. The worker picks up the small black rubber O-ring as a separate loose part and attaches, places, or aligns it onto the blue cap. One O-ring leaves the cluster of spare O-rings resting on the cutting mat, so afterwards that cluster holds one fewer.
Step 2: Focus on what the worker has in their hands. The worker attaches, inserts, or aligns the blue cap into a port of the black manifold by hand.
Step 3: Focus on what the worker has in their hands. The worker uses the wrench to tighten the blue cap on the black manifold.
